🌟 About Me

I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen and advise by Prof. Xuan Wang. My research primarily focuses on algorithmic game theory, multi-agent reinforcement learning, and adversarial team games. My long-term goal is to advance the application of game AI technologies in real-world scenarios, contributing to the technological progress of society. I am expected to graduate in 2025 and am actively seeking job opportunities.

I have interned at Pengcheng Laboratory for one year. Long-term collaborations with Dr. Haobo Fu from Tencent AI Lab and Prof. Kai Li from the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I am actively seeking collaborators with shared interests to apply relevant technologies in scenarios such as imperfect information games, autonomous driving, and large model inference, and to jointly explore these exciting fields.

Looking forward to connecting with like-minded researchers and professionals!
